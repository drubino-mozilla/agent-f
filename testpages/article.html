Why browsers throttle background tabs

By Franklin the Firefox

Browsers slow down timers in tabs you aren't looking at. It saves battery and keeps the tab you are using fast. Most pages never notice.

What changes in the background

  • Timers run at most once a second.
  • Animation frames stop.
  • Nothing is painted.

Pages that pause when hidden, such as some video players, will pause. A Page Visibility API listener is how they find out.

Why it matters for agents

An agent working in a background tab sees the same throttling. It has to wait for the page to settle rather than assume a fixed delay.
